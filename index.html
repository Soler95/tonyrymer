Biography

The Artist

Born in Boston and trained at the New England Conservatory and the Hochschule für Musik Hanns Eisler Berlin, Tony Rymer has appeared as a soloist with the Cleveland Orchestra, the Boston Pops and the Atlanta, Detroit and Pittsburgh symphony orchestras.

A passionate chamber musician, he has performed with Hilary Hahn, Itzhak Perlman, Midori and Kim Kashkashian, and at festivals such as Marlboro, Ravinia and the Perlman Music Program.
